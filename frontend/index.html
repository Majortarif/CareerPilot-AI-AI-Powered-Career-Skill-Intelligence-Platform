Prototype notice

This is a frontend prototype: no backend, no database, no real authentication and no external AI API. AI analysis is simulated with deterministic, rule-based logic and should not be interpreted as real machine-learning or LLM inference. Data is stored only in your browser's LocalStorage.

Features

Everything between "who am I?" and "I got the offer"

Eleven connected tools share one career profile, so every analysis builds on the last.

  • Career Profile

    Education, target role, summary and a skills editor with levels.

  • CV Analyzer

    Skills, strengths, suggestions and missing sections from pasted text or a sample CV.

  • Job Analyzer

    Required vs preferred skills, responsibilities, keywords and experience level.

  • Skill Gap

    🟢 strong, 🟡 partial and 🔴 missing skills with a prototype compatibility score.

  • Skill Intelligence

    A 140-skill dictionary across 7 categories powers extraction and your skill profile.

  • Career Roadmap

    A 12-week plan with objectives, practice, mini projects and hours.

  • Project Recommendations

    Portfolio ideas ranked by how many of your gaps they practise.

  • Application Tracker

    Seven statuses, search, filters, sorting, table and kanban views.

  • Analytics

    Five charts on applications, conversion, skills and learning.

  • AI Assistant

    A rule-based chat that answers with your own saved data.

  • Settings

    Theme, motion, demo data, JSON export/import and reset.

How it works

One flow, from profile to progress

The intended product experience, fully clickable in this prototype.

  1. 1Build your career profile
  2. 2Analyze your CV and extract skills
  3. 3Paste a target job description
  4. 4See strong, partial and missing skills
  5. 5Follow a 12-week learning roadmap
  6. 6Build recommended portfolio projects
  7. 7Track applications through 7 statuses
  8. 8Review career analytics
